How every project runs.

Our commitments aren't a poster on a wall — they're built into the programme. Here's what the journey looks like on every job we take on.

Brief & site review

We document your goals, site constraints and budget before quoting — so the scope we agree is the scope we deliver.

Stage 01 · Site visit, feasibility, written brief

Fixed scope & programme

A written scope, programme and hold-point schedule — with a named supervisor responsible for each stage of the build.

Stage 02 · Contract, programme, inspection plan

Documented build

Inspections at defined hold points throughout construction, with licensed trades operating under our documented safety system.

Stage 03 · Hold-point inspections, site safety, progress updates

Handover & warranty

Completion certificates, statutory warranty documentation and a handover you can check against the brief we wrote together.

Stage 04 · Certificates, warranty, aftercare
