A building company built around straight answers.

ADA Construction Group exists because too many people finish a build wishing they'd been told the truth earlier — about cost, about time, about risk. We structure our whole company around preventing that.

Our story

ADA Construction Group Pty Ltd was founded on a straightforward observation: the residential building industry does not have a communication problem so much as a commitment problem. Builders rarely fail because they can't construct — they fail because clients were promised one thing and delivered another, and nobody explained the gap.

From our first projects we invested in the parts of building that clients never see but always feel: feasibility reviews before contracts, written programmes, inspection hold points, and a named person who answers the phone. Those investments cost us speed early on. They've returned every one of our referrals since.

How we're structured

We deliberately stay a mid-sized company. Large enough to resource your project properly; small enough that the person who quotes your build is contactable while it's under construction.

  • Construction operations — site supervisors and leading hands who run one project at a time.
  • Pre-construction — estimation, procurement and scheduling, separated from sales on purpose.
  • Client liaison — a single named contact for you from contract signature to aftercare.
