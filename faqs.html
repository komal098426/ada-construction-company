The questions every client asks before signing — answered the way we'd answer them across the table.

Budgets & costs

Honestly: it depends — and anyone quoting a reliable price from a website form is guessing. What we can do honestly: an early conversation establishes a realistic range for your scope and site, and that range narrows as documentation firms up through feasibility and pre-construction. The budget conversation is free; the surprise version of it isn't. See why quotes differ for why the same drawings can produce very different prices.

Timelines & process

Timelines depend on scope, site conditions, approvals and selections — so we don't publish generic durations; they'd be misleading. Once feasibility is complete, you receive a programme with stage-by-stage timings that reflect your actual project. See our process for the six stages every build follows.

Every variation — however small, in either direction — is documented, priced and agreed in writing before the work proceeds. Nothing proceeds on a verbal nod. It's slower for us than a shrug and a "she'll be right", which is exactly why we do it.

Working together

You get a named supervisor as your single point of contact for the whole build — not a rotating inbox or whoever's on site that week. Our supervisors run one project at a time so that contact is genuinely available. Communication follows an agreed cadence: scheduled updates, not just calls when something needs sign-off.

Yes — with the caveat that timing matters. Selections and changes are welcome at any point, but each one interacts with the programme: a late tile selection delays waterproofing; a structural change after frame affects engineering, certification and everything downstream. We'll always tell you what a proposed change costs in both dollars and programme days before you decide.
