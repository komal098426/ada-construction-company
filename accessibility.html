Accessibility statement.

Building should be open to everyone — so should a builder's website.

Our commitment

We want every visitor to be able to find information about our services and contact us, regardless of ability, device or assistive technology. This website targets WCAG 2.2 AA practices as the working standard for its design and code.

What that means in practice

  • Full keyboard operability for navigation, forms, filters and FAQ accordions, with a visible focus indicator.
  • A skip link on every page to bypass repeated navigation.
  • Semantic headings, landmarks and form labels wired to their fields, with error messages associated with each input.
  • Colour contrast checked against WCAG AA ratios; no information conveyed by colour alone.
  • Meaningful alternative text for imagery.
  • Reduced-motion support — animations are disabled when your system requests it.
  • Tap targets sized for mobile use, and forms designed to complete on a phone.

Known limitations

This is a draft build: imagery is placeholder illustration and final accessibility testing (keyboard-only walkthroughs, screen-reader passes on major journeys, automated audits) is scheduled as a formal phase before launch. Gaps found in testing will be listed here honestly once known.
