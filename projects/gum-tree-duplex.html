Gum Tree Duplex

A sloping block, two investors, and a council overlay protecting the site's mature eucalypts — a duplex that had to earn its footprint.

Summary

An investment duplex on a sloping growth-corridor site. The developer clients needed the build to fit a fixed feasibility envelope, while the site's significant trees — protected by overlay — shaped where construction could go at all.

Client brief & constraints

  • Brief: Two three-bedroom dwellings, target rents, fixed feasibility window and exit strategy.
  • Constraint — slope: A fall across the block required split-level design and staged retaining.
  • Constraint — trees: Mature eucalypts with protection zones limited both the building envelope and construction access.
  • Constraint — shared structure: The party wall between dwellings had to be built to acoustic and fire standards for future separate titling.

Scope of work

Full construction of both dwellings: split-level slabs, light-weight upper construction to reduce retaining costs, party wall to dual-occupancy standards, services infrastructure for both homes, and completion to tenancy-ready standard.

Approach & key decisions

Tree protection zones were fenced before mobilisation and monitored for the project's duration — the trees dictated the crane positions, material laydown areas and even the plumbing routes. On the slope, we proposed light-weight construction on the upper levels in place of the originally drafted masonry, cutting the retaining-structure cost significantly while meeting the design's streetscape obligation. Both decisions went through the clients' feasibility model before implementation.

Outcome

Both dwellings completed within the agreed programme and leased on completion. Measurable outcomes will be published only if verified and approved by the client.
