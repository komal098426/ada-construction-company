The Quarry Review

Not every project we're proudest of is a build. This one was a review — and the variations it prevented were worth more than most.

Summary

A private client came to us with a full set of drawings, three wildly different builder quotes, and a simple question: "Which one do I believe?" Our pre-construction review was the answer.

Client brief & constraints

  • Brief: An independent builder's review of the documentation and the three quotes before any contract was signed.
  • Constraint — timeline: The client's finance pre-approval had an expiry date; the review had a fixed window.
  • Constraint — stakes: The quote spread was large enough that choosing wrong meant losing the project or the budget.

Scope of work

A full documentation review — drawings against specifications, specifications against quotes — plus a cost-model reconstruction identifying where each quote had assumed, excluded or double-counted, and a written remediation list for the designer.

Approach & key decisions

The quotes weren't wrong so much as they were answers to three different questions: each builder had interpreted the ambiguous documentation differently. We rebuilt the scope line-by-line into a single baseline, then re-quoted each builder against it — the spread narrowed dramatically, and the differences that remained were real differences in inclusions the client could finally compare like-for-like.

Outcome

The client signed with a builder — not us — on documentation that was now complete, at a price they could trust. We consider that a success. Measurable outcomes will be published only if verified and approved by the client.
