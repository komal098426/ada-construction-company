Mill Street Rebuild

Same street, same school, same neighbours — an unliveable house replaced with a home the family designed for the next thirty years.

Summary

A knockdown rebuild for a family who loved their location but had outgrown — and structurally lost confidence in — their existing home. We coordinated demolition and constructed the replacement as one continuous programme under a single contract.

Client brief & constraints

  • Brief: A four-bedroom family home designed for the block, keeping the established garden's mature trees where possible.
  • Constraint — structure: The existing home had significant footing movement; renovation quotes kept converging on rebuild costs.
  • Constraint — neighbours: Both side boundaries had structures close to the fence line; demolition and crane operations needed protection plans.
  • Constraint — timing: The family's rental lease created a fixed occupancy deadline.

Scope of work

Utility disconnections, licensed demolition and site clearing, service relocations, construction of the new two-storey home to approved plans, landscape reinstatement to agreed scope, and handover documentation.

Approach & key decisions

Because the family's lease was a hard deadline, the programme was built backwards from occupancy date — and the demolition contract included daily site-clean provisions so the neighbouring properties experienced a managed site, not a ruin. The old home's footing problems informed the new design: the engineer's solution for the replacement addressed the soil movement the site was known for, rather than repeating the original's assumptions.

Outcome

The family moved in on the date the programme promised — the lease ran out the week after. Measurable outcomes will be published only if verified and approved by the client.
