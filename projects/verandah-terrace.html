Verandah Terrace

Keep the character the street loves. Change everything that made the home hard to live in. Do both at once, on a narrow site, with a family in residence nearby.

Summary

A full renovation and rear extension of a double-fronted heritage terrace. The project brief: preserve and restore the original front rooms, replace a poorly-built 1980s rear with a contemporary extension, and give a family of five room to grow without leaving their neighbourhood.

Client brief & constraints

  • Brief: Restore the heritage front; add three bedrooms, open living and a connection to the small rear garden.
  • Constraint — heritage: Front façade and key interior elements were protected; works needed to satisfy heritage conditions.
  • Constraint — site: A narrow terrace with side access only; no crane or machinery could reach the rear.
  • Constraint — party walls: Two shared walls with occupied neighbouring homes required monitoring and protection.

Scope of work

Structural repair and restoration of the original front rooms, removal of the existing rear, construction of a two-storey extension with new kitchens, bathrooms and living spaces, full rewiring and replumbing, and reinstatement of heritage details to the street façade.

Approach & key decisions

The narrow site made the rear extension a hand-build: materials were handled through side access, and the programme was sequenced around daily neighbour consideration — noise-intensive work was scheduled in agreed windows. Party-wall monitoring was installed before any structural work, with readings shared with both neighbours weekly. Where opening the original structure revealed surprises — as it always does — each was photographed, priced and agreed with the clients before work proceeded.

Materials & design features

  • Reclaimed brick matched to the original façade for the extension's street-facing returns
  • Restored original timber windows front; new timber-framed glazing to the rear
  • Hydronic heating retained in restored front rooms, extended to new spaces
  • Skylight strategy to bring light deep into the narrow terrace plan

Outcome

Heritage conditions were satisfied at inspection, and the family moved back into a home that keeps its street face and works behind it. Measurable outcomes will be published only if verified and approved by the client.
