Headland House

A custom home for a wind-exposed coastal site — where the brief wasn't just what to build, but how to make it liveable.

Summary

A private-client custom home on an exposed coastal block. The design — by the clients' architect — called for a low-slung pavilion with deep sheltered outdoor rooms on the leeward side. Our job was to make it stand up to the site it was drawn for.

Client brief & constraints

  • Brief: A permanent family home for three, designed to entertain and to age in place.
  • Constraint — wind: The site takes full frontal coastal wind exposure; the design needed a robust envelope without feeling like a bunker.
  • Constraint — corrosion: Salt-spray environment required specified fixings and hardware fit for coastal exposure.
  • Constraint — access: A narrow, steep approach limited deliveries and crane options during construction.

Scope of work

Full construction of a four-bedroom pavilion home: site preparation on a cut-and-fill platform, timber frame with engineered connections, coastal-rated cladding and glazing systems, two sheltered outdoor rooms, and full internal fit-out to architect's specification.

Approach & key decisions

Two decisions shaped the build. First, we sequenced the envelope early — weathertightness ahead of schedule meant interior trades could proceed through the storm season without humidity damage. Second, we pre-ordered every long-lead coastal-rated item (glazing systems, hardware, external finishes) at contract, avoiding the lead-time overruns that coastal builds often absorb late in the programme.

Materials & design features

  • Marine-grade external fixings and hardware throughout
  • Dark cladding with expressed jointing to the street façade
  • Stacked-slider glazing to the leeward outdoor rooms
  • Timber linings brought indoors for continuity of palette

Outcome

Delivered to the architect's documentation with defects resolved before handover — the clients moved in at the agreed date. Measurable outcomes (programme variance, defect counts) will be published only if verified and approved by the client.
