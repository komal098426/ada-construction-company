The Workshop Addition

A craftsman-client, a growing home business, and a build that had to work around a family living in it the whole time.

Summary

A rear extension adding a workshop, studio and connecting living space for a family running a design business from home. The clients are makers themselves — which raised the standard every detail had to meet.

Client brief & constraints

  • Brief: A workshop for the family business, a studio, and a connecting living space opening to the garden.
  • Constraint — occupied home: The family lived on site for the whole build; dust, noise and access had to be actively managed.
  • Constraint — standards: The clients' craft meant exposed details had to be genuinely well made — visible joinery, honest connections.
  • Constraint — budget: A defined ceiling with a stated preference: fewer square metres, better made.

Scope of work

A timber-framed rear extension with exposed structure, workshop fit-out including dust extraction and three-phase power, studio level above, connecting living space with garden orientation, and restoration of the adjoining existing rooms disturbed by the works.

Approach & key decisions

Living on site changes a build's texture: every morning starts with a walk-through with the family, and the programme blocked noisy operations into agreed windows with the children's routine. The extension was framed in timber with deliberately exposed connections — the clients reviewed the joinery details at mock-up stage, and their sign-off was a contractual hold point before the visible work proceeded.

Outcome

Handed over with the business operational in the workshop within the first week. Measurable outcomes will be published only if verified and approved by the client.
