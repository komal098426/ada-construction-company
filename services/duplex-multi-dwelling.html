Duplex & multi-dwelling builds

Development projects succeed on cost-planning discipline and programme certainty. We deliver duplex and multi-dwelling builds subject to approved feasibility and planning — with numbers you can take to the bank conversation.

Is this service right for your project?

  • Duplex construction for investment or multi-generational living
  • Townhouse and multi-dwelling developments
  • Knockdown-rebuild-plus-duplex on suitably zoned land
  • Projects with a defined feasibility window and exit strategy

What's included

Included

  • Feasibility review and cost planning
  • Coordination with your design and consultant team
  • Procurement with supply-chain lead times mapped
  • Programmed construction with milestone reporting
  • Securing and site management across dwellings
  • Handover documentation per dwelling

Not included

  • Development design and planning strategy
  • DA / planning approvals on your behalf
  • Finance, marketing or sales of dwellings
  • Strata or subdivision certification
  • Landscaping beyond agreed scope

Typical project considerations

Multi-dwelling projects are feasibility-led: the build needs to fit a number that was set long before construction starts. Our role is to protect that number — through early procurement of long-lead items, tight programming across both dwellings, and reporting that gives you the position whenever you need it, not just at milestones.

Common factors: site coverage and setback rules, shared-wall construction sequencing, services infrastructure capacity, and staging between dwellings.
