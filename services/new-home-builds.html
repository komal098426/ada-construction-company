New home builds

You bring the approved plans and specifications. We bring the programme, the trades and the documented quality checks — and deliver a home that matches what was drawn.

Is this service right for your project?

  • You hold approved plans and specifications for a new residence
  • You've purchased a block and have a builder-ready design
  • You're working with a designer who has completed documentation
  • You want one contracted party responsible for the whole build

What's included

Included

  • Site preparation and demolition where required
  • Full construction to approved documentation
  • Programming, procurement and trade management
  • Inspections at defined hold points
  • Selections coordination for specified items
  • Handover with certificates and warranties

Not included

  • Design or drafting of your plans
  • Planning or building approvals
  • Landscaping beyond agreed scope
  • Finance or loan arrangements
  • Furniture, window treatments, and loose items

Typical project considerations

With approved documentation in hand, the critical factors are site conditions (soil, slope, access), the completeness of the specification, and lead times on selected materials. Our estimate includes a documentation review so gaps between drawings and specifications are found before construction — not during it.
