Pre-construction & construction management

The best time to change a project is before it starts. Our pre-construction service puts a builder's discipline on your side of the table — reviewing, budgeting and coordinating your project into a build-ready state.

Is this service right for your project?

  • You're early-stage and want buildability input before committing
  • You want a realistic budget before signing a construction contract
  • Your project needs consultant coordination — engineer, surveyor, certifier
  • You want an independent builder's review of someone else's documentation
  • You'd like construction managed to your programme and standards

What's included

Included

  • Documentation and buildability review
  • Cost planning and budget development
  • Consultant coordination and procurement planning
  • Programme development and lead-time mapping
  • Selections schedule development
  • Construction management to handover (optional)

Not included

  • Design or drafting services
  • Planning approvals or certification
  • Legal or finance advice
  • Fixed-price guarantees on uncommitted scope
  • Real-estate feasibility or valuation advice

Typical project considerations

Pre-construction is most valuable when there's still room to change things — which means the earlier, the better. Clients typically engage this service while their design is at concept or design-development stage, or when they've received quotes that differ enough to warrant an independent look at why.
