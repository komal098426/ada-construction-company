Custom homes

An individually planned home deserves an individually planned build. We take architect-designed projects from feasibility through to handover with the design intent protected at every stage.

Is this service right for your project?

Custom homes suit clients who have — or want to commission — an architect-designed home, and who value design fidelity over square-metre efficiency.

  • Architect-designed new residences
  • Individually engineered homes for difficult sites
  • High-specification builds with detailed selections
  • Collaborative projects with a client's own design team

What's included

Included

  • Feasibility and site review
  • Buildability review of your design documentation
  • Detailed cost planning and procurement
  • Programming with milestone hold points
  • Selections coordination with your designer
  • Quality inspections at defined stages
  • Handover documentation and warranties

Not included

  • Architectural design itself
  • Planning approvals lodged on your behalf
  • Interior design services (coordinated, not provided)
  • Landscaping beyond agreed scope
  • Off-site works outside the property boundary

Typical project considerations

Custom homes live or die on the quality of the documentation and the alignment between builder, designer and client before construction begins. That's why our custom-home estimates come after a buildability review, not before it — and why we'll tell you when a design detail is going to cost more than it's worth.

Common factors that shape your project: site slope and access, soil classification, wind or bushfire exposure, engineering complexity, and the level of finish specified.

How the build runs

Custom homes follow our full six-stage process, with two additions: a documented buildability review during feasibility, and designer walk-throughs at agreed hold points so design intent is verified — not assumed — during construction. See the full process →
