Knockdown rebuilds

The location you love, the home you actually want. We coordinate demolition and construct your replacement home as one continuous programme — one builder, one schedule, one point of accountability.

Is this service right for your project?

  • Your existing home no longer suits but your street does
  • Renovation costs are approaching rebuild costs
  • You've purchased an unliveable property in an established area
  • You want to stay in the same school catchment or community

What's included

Included

  • Feasibility review of the existing site and services
  • Demolition coordination with licensed contractors
  • Utility disconnections and reconnections
  • Site remediation and preparation
  • Replacement home construction, full programme
  • Handover with certificates and warranties

Not included

  • Design of the replacement home
  • Development or demolition approvals
  • Relocation assistance during construction
  • Removal of hazardous materials beyond agreed scope
  • Boundary or retaining works on neighbouring land

Typical project considerations

The honest answer about a knockdown rebuild usually comes down to three things: what's under the old house (footings, services, contamination risk), what's around it (access, overlay trees, neighbouring structures), and what council allows on the site. Our feasibility review addresses all three in writing before you commit.
