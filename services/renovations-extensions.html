Renovations & extensions

A renovation is an exercise in respecting what exists while changing what matters. We diagnose the existing structure honestly, then alter it deliberately — with liveability managed during construction.

Is this service right for your project?

  • Rear or upper-storey extensions for a growing household
  • Whole-home renovations of tired or dated interiors
  • Structural alterations — wall removal, reconfiguration, lifting
  • Heritage-context upgrades that preserve streetscape character
  • Indoor–outdoor connections and period façade restorations

What's included

Included

  • Existing-structure review and condition assessment
  • Structural coordination with your engineer
  • Staged programming for occupied homes where agreed
  • Protection of retained elements and finishes
  • Full construction of the renovation or addition
  • Inspections at defined hold points, handover documentation

Not included

  • Design and drafting of the renovation
  • Heritage or planning approvals
  • Temporary accommodation for your household
  • Furniture and loose furnishings
  • Asbestos removal beyond identified, agreed scope

Typical project considerations

Renovations carry a variable that new builds don't: the existing structure is full of surprises behind every wall opened. We manage that honestly — with a contingency approach agreed before works begin, and immediate disclosure when a wall cavity tells us something the drawings couldn't.

Other common factors: whether you'll live on site during works (we programme around it), matching original construction details, and protecting mature plantings and neighbouring properties.
