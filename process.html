The journey from first conversation to aftercare.

Six stages. Each with a named owner, defined client inputs, defined outputs — and no gap between them where your project drifts.

1. Initial conversation

We listen first. This stage is about your site, your brief, your desired outcome, and whatever documentation you already hold — drawings, surveys, approvals, or just an idea and a block of land.

Owner: Client liaison  ·  You bring: Your goals, site address, any documentation  ·  Output: A shared understanding of scope and the recommended next step

2. Feasibility & site review

Before anyone quotes anything, we look: site access, ground conditions, planning constraints, design implications, programme realism and budget alignment. You receive the findings in writing.

Owner: Pre-construction lead  ·  You bring: Access to the site, any known constraints  ·  Output: Written feasibility summary with identified risks and considerations

3. Pre-construction planning

Drawings, specifications, consultants, selections and procurement are coordinated into one contract-ready package. This is where budget certainty is actually created.

Owner: Estimator & pre-construction team  ·  You bring: Decisions on design and selections  ·  Output: Contract documentation, programme and cost plan

4. Construction

Site operations, trades, safety and quality checks run to the programme — with inspections at defined hold points and communication on an agreed cadence, not just when something needs sign-off.

Owner: Site supervisor (one project at a time)  ·  You bring: Attendance at agreed walk-throughs, timely selections  ·  Output: Completed construction, inspection records

5. Handover

Inspections, documentation and commissioning are completed before keys change hands. You leave with warranties, certificates and a maintenance guide — not a pile of promises.

Owner: Construction manager  ·  You bring: Final inspection attendance  ·  Output: Keys, handover documentation, warranty certificates

6. Aftercare

Defects, warranty and maintenance all have a named owner and a clear contact route. When something needs attention after handover, you know exactly who to call — and they answer.

Owner: Client liaison  ·  You bring: Any issues, as they arise  ·  Output: Resolved defects, warranty administration

Approximate durations are deliberately omitted. Every project's timeline differs with site, scope, approvals and selections — publishing generic durations would be misleading. Stage timings for your project are provided in your feasibility summary and programme.

What we ask of you

A process only protects a project when both sides work it. Three commitments from you keep the programme honest:

  • Decisions by the date they're needed — selection dates are in the programme because late selections cost programme.
  • One channel of communication — changes and questions go through the named contact so nothing gets lost between site and office.
  • Honesty about budget — we can't protect a number we don't know.

No cost, no obligation, no pressure — just an honest discussion about your site and your goals.
