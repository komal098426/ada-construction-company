What we collect

When you submit an enquiry through this website, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, phone number, project location, project description and any optional details (budget range, timeframe, documents and attachments). We also collect standard technical information through analytics — pages viewed, approximate region, device type — configured to privacy-conscious settings approved by the client.

Why we collect it

Enquiry information is collected for one purpose: responding to your enquiry about a potential construction project. Analytics information is collected to understand how the website is used and improve it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information.
  • We don't add you to marketing lists from a project enquiry without separate consent.
  • We don't send analytics events that include your message content or unnecessary personal data.
